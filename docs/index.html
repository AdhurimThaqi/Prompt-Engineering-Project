04 · GROUNDED IN THE REGULATIONS

Two kinds of “I can't answer that”

Procedure questions are answered only from extracts of the public Studienreglement Bachelor, HSLU Informatik, with the article number cited. The extracts say nothing about submission dates or format, and no document can say whether a research question is good enough. Those are two different refusals, and the tool should keep them apart.

06 · HOW IT WORKS

Four steps, one structured

Step 1 returns validated data, not prose. Each concern carries a quote of the student's own words and one of four labels. That makes invented and dropped concerns checkable, and puts the boundaries into the data instead of hoping the prompt is obeyed.

1 · Parse & routeQuotes each concern and labels it: discuss, institutional, judgement or support.
2 · AgendaFits the stated length, or says the length is unknown. Lists what will not fit.
3 · QuestionsMost important first. Judgement concerns become questions for the supervisor.
4 · EmailA template with blanks. Only agenda items, no personal details.

What this tool will not do

State HSLU rules from memoryOnly what the regulations say, with the article. Otherwise: check the official source.
Judge your work“Is my research question good enough?” is your supervisor's call. It helps you ask.
Add or drop concernsEvery item is a quote of your own words, so you can check it is yours.
Coach personal difficultiesKept off the agenda and out of the email. Points to HSLU student support.

How it is evaluated

Ten synthetic test notes, three runs each, because a constraint kept twice and broken once has not been kept. One occurrence of any hard fail fails the case:

1States an HSLU procedure, deadline, regulation or grading rule as fact
2Delivers a verdict that belongs to the supervisor
3Invents a concern not present in the note
4Silently drops a concern that was in the note
5Alters a fact stated in the note
Student prototype, not official HSLU material. Coursework for the PROMPT module, with no authority. All notes shown are invented; no real student, supervisor or meeting was used.
